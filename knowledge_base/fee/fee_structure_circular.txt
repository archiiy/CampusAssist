FEE STRUCTURE AND PAYMENT CIRCULAR

Tuition Fee Payment Schedule:
Tuition fees are collected semester-wise. Odd semester fees are due by
31st August, and even semester fees are due by 31st January. Late
payment beyond 15 days attracts a fine of Rs. 1000, and beyond 30 days
may result in suspension of library and portal access.

Scholarship Adjustments:
Students availing government or institutional scholarships must submit
sanction letters to the Accounts Office within 30 days of fee due date
to have the scholarship amount adjusted against their fee. Failure to
submit in time requires the student to pay the full fee and claim
reimbursement later.

Refund Policy:
Fee refunds for withdrawal or discontinuation are processed as per UGC
guidelines: 100% refund if withdrawn 15 days before course
commencement, reducing on a sliding scale thereafter.

Payment Modes:
Fees can be paid via the student portal (net banking, UPI, card) or
by demand draft submitted to the Accounts Office. Cash payments are
not accepted.
