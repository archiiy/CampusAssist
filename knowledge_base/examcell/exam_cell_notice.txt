EXAMINATION CELL — NOTICE

Hall Ticket Issuance:
Hall tickets for the end-semester examination are released on the
student portal 5 days before the first exam date, provided the student
has no outstanding fee dues and meets the minimum attendance
requirement of 75%.

Revaluation Process:
Students may apply for revaluation of an answer script within 10 days
of result publication, through the student portal, by paying the
prescribed revaluation fee per subject. Revaluation results are
typically published within 21 working days.

Arrear Examination:
Students with arrears (failed courses) may register for the
supplementary/arrear examination conducted at the end of each semester.
Arrear exam registration closes 15 days before the exam date.

CGPA Calculation:
CGPA is calculated as the credit-weighted average of grade points
across all completed semesters. Grade points range from 0 (F) to 10
(O — Outstanding).
