ACADEMIC HANDBOOK — GENERAL REGULATIONS

Minimum Attendance Requirement:
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course per
semester to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination.
Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation
through the Head of Department, subject to valid medical or other
documented reasons.

Credit System:
The university follows a Choice Based Credit System (CBCS). Each course
carries credits ranging from 1 to 4 based on contact hours. A minimum of
160 credits must be earned to complete the B.E./B.Tech program.

Timetable and Course Registration:
Course registration for each semester opens two weeks before the
semester start date and closes one week after classes begin. Students
must register through the student portal; late registration requires
approval from the class advisor.

Faculty Advisor System:
Each student is assigned a faculty advisor at the start of the program
who must be consulted for academic planning, elective selection, and
addressing academic difficulties.
