PLACEMENT CELL — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Eligibility Criteria:
Students must have a minimum CGPA of 6.5 with no standing arrears to be
eligible for campus placement drives. Some core-company drives may
require a higher CGPA threshold as specified in the individual company's
job description.

Registration Process:
Students must register on the Placement Portal at the start of their
pre-final year. Resume upload and profile completion are mandatory
before a student can apply to any company drive.

Internship Policy:
Students who receive a Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) after an internship
are still required to inform the Placement Cell and complete final
formalities before the offer is confirmed as final placement.

Mock Interview Support:
The Placement Cell conducts mock interview and group discussion
preparation sessions in the semester prior to major recruitment drives.
Students can register for these sessions through the placement office.
