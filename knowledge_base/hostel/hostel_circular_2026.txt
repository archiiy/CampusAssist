HOSTEL CIRCULAR — 2026 ACADEMIC YEAR

Hostel Fee Payment Deadline:
Hostel fees for the odd semester must be paid on or before 30th
September 2026. A late fee of Rs. 500 per week applies after the
deadline. Students who have not cleared hostel dues by 15th October
2026 risk losing their allotted room.

Room Allotment:
Room allotment is done by the Hostel Warden's office based on seniority
and application date. Room change requests must be submitted in writing
to the warden and are processed within 7 working days.

Mess Timings:
Breakfast: 7:30 AM – 9:00 AM
Lunch: 12:30 PM – 2:00 PM
Dinner: 7:30 PM – 9:00 PM
Mess fee is billed separately and must be paid monthly by the 5th of
each month.

Warden Contact:
For any hostel-related grievance, students should first approach their
respective floor warden before escalating to the Chief Warden's office.
